Frequently Asked Support Questions

When does my daily evaluation quota reset?

Your 15 daily answer evaluations reset every night at midnight (00:00 AM IST) automatically. You start each morning with a full 15-copy allowance.

Are my past uploaded copies saved?

Yes! When logged in with your Google account, all evaluated copies and detailed margin scorecards are securely preserved in "My Answer Locker" for easy review.
