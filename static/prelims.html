Frequently Asked Questions on UPSC CSE Prelims Strategy

Evidence-backed insights on negative marking, safe attempt ranges, and error tracking.

What is the optimal question attempt range for UPSC CSE Prelims? ▾

For General category cutoffs (typically 85–95 marks in recent years), most successful candidates attempt between 78 and 88 questions. Attempting fewer than 70 requires unrealistically high accuracy (>85%), while attempting over 92 with low 50:50 confidence leads to severe negative marking penalty (-0.66 per blunder). CookedMains computes your personalized Expected Value ($EV$) so you know your exact optimal cutoff ceiling.

Why do commercial mock tests differ from actual UPSC Prelims questions? ▾

Many commercial test series rely on factual triviality and extreme statement tricks rather than core conceptual mechanics. Actual UPSC past papers test constitutional doctrines, economic principles, and interdisciplinary causal relationships. Practicing on 100% verified UPSC past questions prevents false exam panic.

How does the Self-Clearing Mistakes Notebook help retention? ▾

Passive reading of 80-page mock test solution PDFs has a low 10-day retention rate. Active recall testing with an auto-updating mistake ledger forces your brain to retrieve the correct distinction. When you answer the question correctly upon re-test, it vanishes from your error ledger, gamifying your journey to zero mistakes.
