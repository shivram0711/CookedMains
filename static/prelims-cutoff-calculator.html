Frequently Asked Questions (Prelims Cutoff & EV Strategy)

How many questions should I attempt in GS Paper 1? ▼

Most successful candidates attempt between 82 and 92 questions. Because very few candidates know more than 40-45 questions with absolute certainty, disciplined 50:50 elimination across an additional 35-45 questions is statistically mandatory to clear the 85-92 mark threshold.

What is the negative marking penalty in GS Paper 1? ▼

Each question in GS Paper 1 carries 2.00 marks. Every incorrect response subtracts 1/3rd of the question marks (-0.66 marks). Unattempted questions carry zero penalty.
