What is the Safe Attempt Target for UPSC CSAT Paper 2?

To clear the mandatory 33% qualifying cutoff (66.67 out of 200 marks) in UPSC CSAT Paper-2, an aspirant needs a net score of at least 67 marks. Because each wrong answer attracts a strict -0.833 mark negative penalty, attempting too few questions (e.g. 30 questions) leaves zero margin for error. A safe, mathematically resilient strategy targets 42 to 48 disciplined attempts at 75% accuracy to guarantee a net score above 75 marks.

Frequently Asked Questions (UPSC CSAT Cutoff & Strategy)

What happens if I score above 120 in GS Paper 1 but get 66.00 in CSAT? ▼

If an aspirant fails to achieve 66.67 marks in CSAT Paper 2, they are automatically disqualified from UPSC Prelims regardless of their score in GS Paper 1. GS-1 marks are not considered in the merit list unless Paper-2 is qualified.

Why has CSAT become more difficult since 2023? ▼

Since 2023, UPSC increased the difficulty of Quantitative Aptitude (Number Systems, Permutations, Modular Arithmetic) and shortened the length of Reading Comprehension passages while increasing inference ambiguity. A safe attempt strategy balancing both sections is now essential.
