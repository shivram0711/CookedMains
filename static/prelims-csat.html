What is the safe attempt target for UPSC CSAT Paper 2?

To clear the mandatory 33% qualifying cutoff (66.67 out of 200 marks) in UPSC CSAT Paper-2, an aspirant needs a net score of at least 67 marks. Because each wrong answer attracts a strict -0.833 mark negative penalty, attempting too few questions (e.g. 30 questions) leaves zero margin for error. A safe strategy targets 42 to 48 disciplined attempts at 75% accuracy to guarantee a net score above 75 marks.

Frequently Asked Questions: UPSC CSAT Paper 2 Cutoff

What happens if I score high in GS1 but fail CSAT? ▼

If a candidate scores less than 66.67 marks in CSAT (Paper-2), they are automatically disqualified from UPSC Prelims. Their General Studies Paper-1 marks will not be evaluated for the Prelims merit cutoff, regardless of whether they scored 130+ marks in GS Paper-1.

What is the marks distribution in UPSC CSAT? ▼

UPSC CSAT contains exactly 80 objective multiple-choice questions for 200 maximum marks. Each question is worth +2.5 marks. Each incorrect answer attracts a negative penalty of 1/3rd (-0.833 marks). Unattempted questions incur 0 penalty.

Which section in CSAT carries the most marks? ▼

Every question in CSAT carries identical marks (+2.5 marks). Historically, CSAT is divided into Reading Comprehension (approx. 27 questions), Quantitative Aptitude (approx. 35 questions), and Logical & Analytical Reasoning (approx. 18 questions).
