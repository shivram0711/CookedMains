Frequently Asked Questions (Prelims Mock Tests)

How are scorecards generated after completing a mock test? ▼

For any mock or drill of 5 or more questions, CookedMains computes your net score (+2.00 per correct, -0.66 per incorrect), accuracy percentage, subject breakdown, and behavioral error classification (Knowledge Gap vs Slipped Read vs Overconfidence).

What if I get interrupted during a 2-hour simulation? ▼

Our Progress Tracker engine automatically preserves your in-progress mock state in localStorage. You can safely return to your session anytime from the Progress Tracker tab and resume right where you left off.
