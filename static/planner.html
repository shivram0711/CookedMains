Prelims MCQ · 2023 · 2 marks Official UPSC Key

With reference to the Election Commission of India, consider the following statements:
1. The Election Commissioners have the same status and receive salary and perks as available to Judges of the Supreme Court.
2. The Chief Election Commissioner cannot be removed from his office except in like manner and on like grounds as a Judge of the Supreme Court.
3. An Election Commissioner cannot be removed from office except on the recommendation of the Chief Election Commissioner.
Which of the statements given above are correct?

Topper's Trap Detector: UPSC frequently tests the fallacy that the CEC receives higher salary than other ECs (they are completely equal under law).
