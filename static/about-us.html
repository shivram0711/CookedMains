Our Mission & Purpose

Democratizing UPSC Mains Answer Evaluation for Every Aspirant

Cooked Mains was born out of a stark realization: the biggest barrier to clearing the UPSC Civil Services Examination is not access to study material, but the lack of fast, honest, and affordable answer evaluation.

The Old Coaching Monopoly

  • Exorbitant Costs: Mains test series routinely cost between ₹25,000 and ₹60,000, shutting out deserving self-study aspirants.
  • 10-14 Day Delays: By the time a student gets their copy back, they have already moved on to other subjects, ruining feedback loops.
  • Generic Feedback: Clichéd remarks like "Improve handwriting" or "Read more current affairs" provide zero actionable insight.
  • Inflated Grades: Artificial high scores that breed dangerous complacency before actual exam day.

The Cooked Mains Standard

  • 100% Free Daily Access: 15 free evaluations every single day for all aspirants across India.
  • Sub-30-Second Turnaround: Powered by cutting-edge Google Gemini 2.0 Multimodal Vision calibrated to UPSC examiner rubrics.
  • Authentic Red-Pen Annotations: Line-by-line margin feedback directly on your handwritten answer script.
  • Strict UPSC Bell Curve: Realistic scoring that holds you to ranker benchmarks and highlights missing dimensions.

Our Philosophy: "Let Me Cook"

In Internet culture, "Let Him Cook" means giving someone the room, resources, and trust to show what they are capable of achieving. In the grueling marathon of UPSC CSE, self-study aspirants don't need spoon-feeding—they need an objective mirror that lets them practice relentlessly, experiment with answer structures, fail safely in mock drills, and reach top-score perfection.
