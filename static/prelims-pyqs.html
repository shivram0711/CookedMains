Frequently Asked Questions (UPSC CSE Prelims PYQs)

How many years of PYQs are recommended for UPSC Prelims? ▼

At least 15 to 16 years (2011 to 2026) are strongly recommended. 2011 marked the modern CSAT and revised GS pattern. Testing 16 years covers all recurring constitutional articles, economic indicators, and environment conventions that UPSC repeats with altered wordings.

How are CDS and CAPF questions beneficial for UPSC CSE? ▼

UPSC creates question banks from common academic panels. Questions on physical geography, modern history acts, and science & technology frequently migrate between CDS, CAPF, and CSE Prelims with slight difficulty modifications.

What is Topper's Elimination Technique? ▼

Rather than searching for the correct option directly, Topper elimination identifies examiner traps: extreme keywords ("all", "only", "drastically"), interchangeable statutory definitions, and false cause-and-effect claims to systematically eliminate 2 distractors and secure a positive expected score.
