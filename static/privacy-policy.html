Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately for all Cooked Mains users

Our Non-Negotiable Commitment: Cooked Mains will never sell, rent, or lease your handwritten answer sheets, personal identity, or performance logs to third-party commercial coaching institutes, lead brokers, or marketing networks.

1. Information We Collect

To provide instant multimodal answer evaluation, we collect and process only the necessary information:

  • Account Information: When signing in with Google OAuth, we receive your email address, profile name, and avatar image. We generate a unique Aspirant Cadet ID to track your daily quota.
  • Uploaded Answer Copies: Photos (JPEG, PNG, WEBP) and PDF files containing handwritten answer sheets that you submit for optical character recognition and evaluation.
  • Evaluation & Mark Records: Scores, examiner red-pen margin comments, rubric breakdowns, and model answers generated for your answer sheets, stored in your personal "My Answer Locker".
  • Technical & Log Data: Browser user agent, device screen dimensions, IP address, and timestamps to prevent bot misuse, manage session continuity, and enforce fair-use daily limits.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to deliver and improve the educational evaluation experience:

  • Multimodal AI Scoring: Processing your handwritten images via secure vision models to detect structure, handwriting, diagrams, and content against official UPSC CSE rubrics.
  • Locker & Marks Tracker: Populating your historical scorecard so you can review feedback, monitor score recovery, and compare past answers over time.
  • Fair Quota Management: Enforcing the 15 free daily answer sheet evaluations per candidate to prevent API exhaustion and ensure platform availability for all students.
  • Platform Enhancement: Diagnosing evaluation accuracy, eliminating transcription errors, and improving model response quality.

3. Security & Answer Confidentiality

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) for all data in transit between your browser and our servers. Uploaded files and evaluation records are stored in secure databases with strict access controls. Only automated AI vision pipelines parse your answer sheets during active evaluation.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To power our cutting-edge capabilities, we collaborate with vetted cloud providers under strict enterprise data-handling terms:

  • Google Identity Services & Google Cloud: For secure 1-click Google OAuth authentication and Gemini 2.0 Multimodal Vision APIs.
  • Supabase / Cloud Database: For encrypted persistence of user lockers and transaction receipts.
  • Cloudflare & Render: For global edge CDN caching, DDoS protection, and secure server hosting.

5. Your Rights & Data Control

As a Cooked Mains aspirant, you maintain complete ownership of your academic work:

  • Access & Review: You can view all past evaluated copies, scorecards, and margin notes inside "My Answer Locker" anytime.
  • Deletion Requests: You may request the permanent removal of your account, evaluation records, or uploaded images by contacting support.
  • Local Storage Clearing: You can clear local browser cache and saved preferences (such as dark/light mode) anytime via your browser settings.
